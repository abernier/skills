The doctrine becomes skills.

Eight skills — `doc-routing`, `module-layout`, `typescript-conventions`,
`react-conventions`, `test-conventions`, `visual-debugging`, `gates`, `pre-1-0`
— carrying the conventions an `AGENTS.md` used to spell out in full.

The file kept the rule and the reason together, and paid for both on every task,
in every repo that copied it. Split, the rule stays where the other agents read
it — one line, the statement alone — and the reason lives here, loaded when the
work actually touches it. What was three hundred lines of always-on prose becomes
thirty lines of index plus a skill that fires on its own subject.

Nothing here names a repo. A product stance, a schema shape, a gate's real
command name: those stay in the repo that owns them.

Nothing here re-states `mattpocock-skills` either. `doc-routing` owns one row of
its table — a convention goes in the always-loaded file — and hands the other two
to `domain-modeling`, which already owns where a context doc and a decision record
live and when one is worth writing. It names no path of its own, so a repo that
never ran that setup still routes correctly.
